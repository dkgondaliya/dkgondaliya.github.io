Research

Hardware forensics & wireless security research

Efficacy & Limitations of ISP / Chip-Off on Hardware-Encrypted Android

2025–26

A comparative, component-level study of why file-based encryption and the Titan M secure enclave invalidate traditional memory forensics on the Google Pixel 5a. It shows that destructive chip-off only yields ciphertext on modern devices, and proposes constructive hardware repair reviving the device so its own hardware decrypts as the viable route to evidence.

IIoT Wi-Fi Deauthentication Resilience

2023–25

Research into the resilience of Industrial IoT networks to IEEE 802.11 deauthentication attacks. The work builds a tool that tests and passively detects these attacks, parses RSN information elements, and analyses whether WPA2/WPA3 Protected Management Frames (PMF) actually defend against them. It extends Dan McInerney’s wifijammer with a passive detector and PMF/WPA3 resilience analysis of my own.
